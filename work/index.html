01 · HealthtechGrowth · Platforms · Brand and Content

An AI platform for children with neurodevelopmental differences

One platform, three audiences, one view of the numbers

The problem

The platform sells to therapy centres, schools and parents, and each one buys for different reasons. A single website and one message were trying to speak to all three, and paid search had no pages built to convert.

Our perspective

Treat each audience as its own market. Start paid search where the buying decision is clearest, centres and schools, and reach parents through search and social instead of ads.

What we built
  • A rebuilt website with a page for each audience
  • Landing pages with one call to action and no menu to leak clicks
  • Google Search campaigns for centres and schools
  • On-page and off-page SEO, with Indian directory listings
  • Weekly social posts on LinkedIn, Instagram and X, illustrated in the brand’s own character style
  • A marketing dashboard that refreshes every evening

Every audience now lands on a page written for it, and the client sees search, ads and social in one dashboard.

02 · HealthtechGrowth · Platforms

A healthtech investor running an accelerator programme

Bringing founders from four European markets into one programme

The problem

The accelerator wanted applications from the UK, Germany, the Netherlands and Sweden, without a local team in any of them, and its website did not yet feel like the programme it was selling.

Our perspective

Run each country as its own campaign with its own message, and make the website do the convincing, so every campaign has somewhere credible to land.

What we built
  • A new website with an immersive 3D identity
  • Structured data so AI search tools describe the programme correctly
  • A market-by-market lead generation plan for four countries
  • Landing pages and messaging shaped for each market

The programme has one site to send every market to, and a campaign plan that can switch countries on and off independently.

03 · Venture capitalBrand and Content · Platforms · Growth

A growth-stage venture fund raising its next fund

One fund story, told the same way everywhere

The problem

The fund needed to speak to limited partners, founders and international audiences at once. Its deck, website, posts and talks had grown up separately and told slightly different stories.

Our perspective

Write the story once, then carry it into every format, so an investor hears the same thesis on a stage, in a deck, in their inbox and in their feed.

What we built
  • The fund pitch deck and a narrative deck for limited partners
  • Keynote decks for international AI and founder events
  • A new website built to the same visual language
  • A monthly investor newsletter
  • A research-led social series
  • An identity for an invite-only webinar series, and an analytics dashboard

Every channel now carries one thesis, and the team reviews the whole programme from a single monthly dashboard.

04 · Venture capitalBrand and Content · Platforms

A fully subscribed early-stage venture fund

Keeping a closed fund’s portfolio in front of investors

The problem

With its fund closed, the firm still wanted its portfolio companies seen, and wanted investors pointed towards the founding team’s next fund.

Our perspective

Let the portfolio do the talking. A steady series of showcases and news posts keeps the firm visible without asking anyone for money.

What we built
  • A portfolio showcase infographic series
  • News posts that recreate each company’s real product screens
  • A monthly newsletter of portfolio highlights
  • A website redesign that points investors to the second fund

The firm publishes a regular, recognisable series built from its portfolio’s own news.

05 · EducationGrowth · Brand and Content

A free revision platform for UK medical students

Making a library of clinical guides findable, including in AI answers

The problem

Hundreds of well-written summaries of UK clinical guidelines were hard to find in search, and the client wanted to appear in Google’s AI Overviews for the questions students actually ask.

Our perspective

Fix the foundations before writing more. Then structure every guide so search engines and AI assistants can lift a clear answer and credit the source.

What we built
  • A full SEO audit and a 90-day fix plan, delivered as an interactive document
  • On-page and off-page SEO across the redesigned site
  • Guides structured for AI answers
  • Social content for students on LinkedIn, Instagram and Facebook, with quizzes run as Instagram Stories

The library now has a clear technical base and a weekly content rhythm aimed at the exams students are sitting.

06 · Deep techBrand and Content · Growth

An Indian maker of a bionic hand

Repositioning a bionic hand as Physical AI

The problem

The product competes with imported devices at a fraction of the price, but was being seen as just another prosthetic. It needed to reach patients, clinicians and investors with one clear idea.

Our perspective

Position the hand for what it really is: a device that reads muscle signals and adapts to each wearer. That story works for a patient, a clinician and an investor alike.

What we built
  • A six-month marketing programme with a review after month one
  • A repositioning around Physical AI
  • Content and conversion assets for each audience
  • Paid media to put the story in front of the right people

One positioning now runs through every piece of content, built to be tested and adjusted month by month.

07 · Professional servicesBrand and Content

The founder of a consulting firm who also leads an industry body

Turning a leader’s real record into a LinkedIn presence

The problem

She had a published book, arbitration and board credentials and decades of work with small businesses, but her LinkedIn presence did not reflect any of it.

Our perspective

Do not invent a persona. Build content pillars from what she has actually done and said, and publish on a steady rhythm.

What we built
  • A LinkedIn personal branding plan
  • Content pillars drawn from her own work and public appearances
  • A posting rhythm and formats she can sustain

Her profile is now planned around four themes she can speak to with authority.

08 · Sports mediaPlatforms

A sports media platform

Finding and closing a hidden route around a site’s firewall

The problem

The platform wanted an independent check of its website before a server handover. Its traffic was meant to pass through a protective network layer.

Our perspective

Look at the site from the outside first, the way an attacker would, then go server by server during the handover.

What we built
  • An external audit in phase one
  • A server-level review and handover in phase two
  • A formal audit report

The audit found the real server address exposed, which let traffic bypass the protection. That route was flagged as the critical fix.
