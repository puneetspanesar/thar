Cookie Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

Our approach

This site does not use advertising cookies. We use Google Analytics cookies to count visits and see which pages are useful, so we can improve the site.

Google Analytics

Google Analytics sets cookies such as _ga, which last up to two years, to tell visits apart. Visitors in the UK and Europe are asked first and analytics cookies are only set if they accept. Everywhere else, analytics runs by default.

Your choice

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time, or use the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. To change a choice you made on this site, clear this site's data in your browser and you will be asked again.

Third parties

Forms are delivered by FormSubmit when you choose to send one.

Questions
