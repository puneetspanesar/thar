About Thar

Thirteen years of growth work, now AI-native

We started as a creative studio and grew into something closer to an engineering team for marketing. Today we bring brand, growth, platforms and AI together, with agents in our own work as much as our clients’.

What we believe

Four rules we never bend

They shape how we plan, how we use AI and how we report to you.

  1. Clear thinking firstEvery engagement starts with a written point of view on what will move your numbers.
  2. One system, not many vendorsBrand, growth, platforms and AI planned together, so every piece of work makes the others stronger.
  3. People in charge of AIAgents do the heavy lifting. People make the calls, check the facts and sign off everything that goes out.
  4. Measured every weekYou see what we did, what it returned and what we will change, every single week.

Where we work

Mumbai studio, clients across India, the UK and Europe

Most of our work happens remotely, with in-person workshops and events where they matter. Senior strategists, designers and engineers lead every engagement, supported by AI agents for research, production and reporting.
