About Thar

An AI-native marketing and technology studio. Thirteen years of building brands, growth programmes and platforms, now run on a connected engine of strategists and AI agents.

Work with us

We started as a creative studio and grew into something closer to an engineering team for marketing. Today we bring brand, growth, platforms and AI together, and we use AI agents across our own work as much as our clients'.

What we believe

01Clear thinking firstEvery engagement starts with a point of view on what will move your numbers, written down and agreed.
02One system, not many vendorsBrand, growth, platforms and AI planned together, so every piece of work makes the others stronger.
03People in charge of AIAgents do the heavy lifting. People make the calls, check the facts and sign off everything that goes out.
04Measured every weekYou see what we did, what it returned and what we will change, every single week.

Where we work

Our studio is in Mumbai, and we work with clients across India, the UK and Europe. Most of our work happens remotely, with in-person workshops and events where they matter.

How we are set up

Senior strategists, designers and engineers lead every engagement, supported by AI agents for research, production and reporting. You get the attention of a boutique and the output of a much larger team.
