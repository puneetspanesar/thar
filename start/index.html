Questions

Questions we often hear

How quickly will we see results from SEO?

Technical fixes and quick wins usually show up in search data within four to eight weeks. Rankings for competitive terms take three to six months of steady content and links. We report movement every week, so you can see the trend before the traffic arrives.

Do you create full brand identities or only campaigns?

Both. We build identities from strategy and naming through to logo, type, colour and guidelines, then run the campaigns and content that keep the brand in market after launch.

What is an AI agent in marketing?

Software that carries out a defined task on its own, such as researching competitors, drafting posts or pulling the weekly numbers. A person sets its goal and checks its output.

Which platforms do you build websites on?

WordPress, Webflow, headless setups and fast static sites, depending on who will update the site and how quickly it needs to load. We recommend a platform once we understand your team.

Do you run events outside Mumbai?

Yes. We plan and run events, exhibitions and activations across India, and work with partners for events in the UK and Europe.
