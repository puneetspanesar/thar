Questions

Questions we often hear

Which platforms do you build websites on?

WordPress, Webflow, headless setups and fast static sites, depending on who will update the site and how quickly it needs to load. We recommend a platform once we understand your team.

How long does a corporate website take?

A typical corporate site takes eight to twelve weeks from kickoff to launch. Landing pages and microsites usually take two to four weeks.

Will the site be ready for search engines and AI assistants?

Yes. Every site ships with clean structure, fast pages, structured data, sitemaps and analytics, so search engines and AI assistants can read and cite it from day one.

Do you look after the site after launch?

Yes. Our care plans cover hosting, updates, security, backups, small changes and a monthly performance report.
