Questions

Questions we often hear

How quickly will we see results from SEO?

Technical fixes and quick wins usually show up in search data within four to eight weeks. Rankings for competitive terms take three to six months of steady content and links. We report movement every week, so you can see the trend before the traffic arrives.

Do you manage ad budgets on Google, Meta and LinkedIn?

Yes. We plan, build and run campaigns on Google, Meta and LinkedIn, and report spend, leads and cost per lead every week. Budgets stay in your own ad accounts, so you keep full ownership.

What is AI search visibility?

It is how often your brand is named when buyers ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google AI Overviews for advice. We track those answers, find the sources they rely on and work to get your brand cited in them.

Can you work with our existing CRM?

Yes. We work with HubSpot, Zoho, Salesforce and most other CRMs, and connect them to your ads and analytics so every lead can be traced back to where it came from.
