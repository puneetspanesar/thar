Agents and workflows that take the repetitive work off your team

Questions

Questions we often hear

What is an AI agent in marketing?

Software that carries out a defined task on its own, such as researching competitors, drafting posts or pulling the weekly numbers. A person sets its goal and checks its output.

Is our data safe?

We use AI tools configured not to train on your data, limit access to what each workflow needs and document where every piece of data goes. We can also work entirely inside your own accounts.

Where should we start?

Most teams start with our AI readiness audit. In two weeks we map your workflows, pick the three that will save the most time and build the first one, so you can see the result before going further.

Will AI replace our marketing team?

No. It takes the repetitive work off them. The teams we work with spend more of their time on strategy, relationships and judgement, and less on formatting, reporting and copying between tools.
