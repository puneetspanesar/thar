Experiences

Events, activations and print that bring the brand into the room

Events, activations, exhibitions and print that carry the digital work into rooms, campuses and cities.

Talk to us about experiences

Some of the best marketing still happens face to face. We plan and produce the moments where people meet your brand in person, and we connect them back to everything happening online.

Questions

Questions we often hear

Do you run events outside Mumbai?

Yes. We plan and run events, exhibitions and activations across India, and work with partners for events in the UK and Europe.

Can you handle print production?

Yes. We design and manage print from reports and brochures to signage and packaging, and check proofs and colour before anything goes to press.

Do you brand offices and campuses?

Yes. We design wayfinding, wall graphics, meeting rooms and welcome areas, and manage installation with trusted vendors.

How do you measure an event?

We agree the goal first, whether that is meetings booked, leads captured or attendance, and set up tracking before the doors open. You get a report within a week of the event closing.
