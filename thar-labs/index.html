Thar Labs

Research that moves ahead of the market

Thar Labs is our research and learning unit. We study how AI, search and B2B buying are changing, test what is new before our clients need it, and share what we learn.

Get the research first

Marketing changes every month now. Thar Labs makes sure the advice we give is based on what is true today, not on what worked last year.

What Thar Labs does

How we stay ahead

Every week, the same rhythm: track, test, understand and share.

TrackWe read the new research, platform updates and buyer studies as they land, and log what changes for our clients’ markets.
TestWe try new tools, ad formats and AI workflows on our own channels first, so nothing reaches a client untested.
UnderstandWe turn what we find into a clear view of what matters, what is noise and what to do about it.
TeachFindings become playbooks and training for our team, so every strategist works from the same current picture.
PublishThe best of it goes out as reports, benchmarks and insights for the leaders we work with.
RefreshPlaybooks are reviewed as the market moves. What worked last year is checked again before we use it.
