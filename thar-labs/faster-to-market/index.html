Faster to market

How B2B firms can use AI to speed up go-to-market and ad campaigns, and where it still needs people in charge.

Key findings

Six findings for B2B leaders

Drawn from the latest research by 6sense, Forrester, Gartner, McKinsey, BCG, Pew and others, published in 2025 and 2026.

82%Decided before contactBuying groups pick the vendor they already favoured before speaking to sales 82% of the time.6sense, 2026
61%Buyers prefer to go rep-freeMost B2B buyers would rather research and buy without a sales rep, and 73% avoid irrelevant outreach.Gartner, 2025
22People shape each purchase13 internal and 9 external participants influence a typical B2B purchase.Forrester, 2026
8% vs 15%Answers are replacing clicksSearchers click a result in 8% of visits when a Google AI summary appears, against 15% without one.Pew Research Center, 2025
15.3%AI budgets are risingCMOs put 15.3% of marketing budgets into AI, yet only 30% say their teams are ready to scale it.Gartner, 2026
5%Few get value at scaleOnly 5% of companies achieve AI value at scale, while 60% report little or none.BCG, 2025

Inside the report

From research to a working plan

The first half sets out how B2B buying, AI adoption and search have changed, with every number sourced. The second half is a playbook: how people and AI agents split the work, how to run AI-assisted ad campaigns, what to measure, six governance rules, a 90-day plan and a readiness checklist.
