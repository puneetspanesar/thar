Last updated November 18, 2026

Our commitment

We want this site to be usable by everyone, and we aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.2 at level AA.

What we have done

Clear headings, keyboard navigation, visible focus states, text alternatives, strong colour contrast and support for reduced motion settings.

Feedback

If anything on this site is hard to use, please tell us at hello@thar.co and we will fix it.
