Why leaders should post before the company page

People follow people. A simple plan to turn three leaders into your strongest channel on LinkedIn.

Company pages on LinkedIn are useful, but they are rarely loved. Posts from people travel further and start more conversations, and buyers want to know who they would actually be working with.

Start with three people

You do not need every leader posting. Pick three who are close to customers, have opinions and can spare an hour a week: often the founder or CEO, the head of sales or a practice lead, and one subject expert. Give each a clear lane so they are not saying the same things.

A rhythm people can keep

  • Two posts a week per leader is plenty. Consistency beats volume.
  • A monthly thirty-minute interview gives us enough material for a month of posts in their own words.
  • Mix formats: a short opinion, a story from a client conversation, a simple chart, an occasional longer article.
  • Leaders reply to comments themselves. That is where the relationships form.

Let the company page amplify

Once leaders are posting, the company page becomes the place that collects and amplifies their best ideas, along with news, hiring and events. It stops shouting into the void and starts pointing at people buyers already trust.

What to measure

Followers and likes are the least interesting numbers. Watch for profile visits from target accounts, inbound messages, mentions in sales calls and the number of conversations that start with "I saw your post".
